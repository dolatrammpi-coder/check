Patwari Special Recruitment Examination (PwD Category) 2026 Recruitment Online Form

Organization: Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB), Bhopal
Post: Patwari Special Recruitment Examination (PwD Category)
Advertisement: Group-02 Subgroup-04 Combined Recruitment Examination-2026
Short Information: MPESB has issued the Group-02 Subgroup-04 Combined Recruitment Examination-2026 rulebook for Patwari Special Recruitment Examination (PwD Category). The rulebook lists 200 Patwari posts.
Important: This Patwari Special Recruitment Examination is for the PwD category. The rulebook states that all 200 posts are advertised as UR/NIL/OPEN and that applicants from all categories may apply. Aadhaar registration is mandatory; carry the prescribed original photo identity document and Aadhaar / e-Aadhaar / Aadhaar details for the examination.

  • Age relaxation: For permanent residents of Madhya Pradesh, as per the rulebook and applicable Madhya Pradesh Government orders.

Patwari Special Recruitment Examination (PwD Category) Recruitment 2026 : Vacancy Details

Post Vacancy Qualification
Patwari (PwD Category) — Total 200 Posts
  • All 200 posts are advertised as UR/NIL/OPEN; applicants from all categories may apply.
  • The rulebook gives the district-wise vacancy table for these PwD-category posts.
  • Indian citizen and a local resident of Madhya Pradesh.
  • Bachelor's degree from a recognized university.
  • CPCT certificate with Hindi typing and computer proficiency is mandatory. If a selected candidate has not passed CPCT, it must be passed within the 3-year probation period after appointment; otherwise the appointment will be cancelled.
  • Valid disability-category certificate issued by the competent authority and registration in Madhya Pradesh employment registration are mandatory.
Vacancy Breakup — EH 94 Posts As listed in the rulebook vacancy table.
Vacancy Breakup — LD 45 Posts As listed in the rulebook vacancy table.
Vacancy Breakup — MD 61 Posts As listed in the rulebook vacancy table.

Patwari Special Recruitment Examination (PwD Category) Selection Process

  • Only candidates obtaining at least 40% of the prescribed total marks will be included in the successful candidates list.
  • Candidates must indicate district preferences in the application; district preferences cannot be changed after submission.
  • MPESB will allot the district on merit and the district preferences entered in the application; the merit list is the first counselling selection list.
  • After document verification, eligible candidates will undergo one counselling process in the allotted district.

Note: चयन प्रक्रिया की अंतिम शर्तें आधिकारिक notification के अनुसार लागू होंगी।

Patwari Special Recruitment Examination (PwD Category) Exam Pattern

Stage Details
Paper I — General Science, General Hindi, General English and General Mathematics 100 Marks
Paper I — General Knowledge and Aptitude, General Computer Knowledge, General Logical Ability and General Management 100 Marks
Online examination response-marking time 3 Hours
Responsive Advertisement Space (AdSense)

Important Instructions

  • Aadhaar registration is mandatory.
  • Bring one prescribed original photo identity document to the examination: voter ID, PAN card, Aadhaar card, driving licence or passport. e-Aadhaar is valid only after UIDAI verification.
  • Bring Aadhaar card / e-Aadhaar card / Aadhaar copy / Aadhaar number / Aadhaar VID details. Biometric verification is mandatory at entry and during the examination; Aadhaar must be unlocked for verification.
  • Entry is permitted only up to the reporting time. Mobile phones, calculators, log tables, digital watches, sunglasses and copying material are prohibited in the examination room.
  • Carry a black ballpoint pen and the admit card downloaded from the Board website.
