BPSSC Forest Range Officer Selection Process

  • Stage 1: Written Competitive Examination (Paper-1 General Hindi & Paper-2 General Studies)
  • Stage 2: Interview / साक्षात्कार (50 Marks) - 6 times candidates called for interview based on written test
  • Stage 3: Physical Standard & Efficiency Test (PST / PET) - Qualifying Nature
  • Stage 4: Medical Examination & Document Verification
