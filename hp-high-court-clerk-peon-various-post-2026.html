HP High Court Selection Process

  • Court Manager: Written Test & Viva-voce (Interview).
  • Clerk: Screening Test, Written Test, Typing Test (English & Hindi) & Document Verification.
  • Stenographer Gr-III: Screening Test, Stenography Test, Typing Test & Document Verification.
  • Driver: Screening Test, Driving Proficiency Test & Document Verification.
  • Process Server: Screening Test, Written Test & Evaluation / Document Verification.
  • Peon / Chowkidar / Safai Karamchari: Merit based on 10+2 marks (85 marks) + Document Evaluation (15 marks).
