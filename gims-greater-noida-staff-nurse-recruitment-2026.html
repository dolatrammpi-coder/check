GIMS Staff Nurse Selection Process

  • Stage 1: कम्प्यूटर आधारित परीक्षा (Computer Based Test - CBT)
  • Stage 2: बायोमेट्रिक एवं दस्तावेज सत्यापन (Biometric & Document Verification)
  • Stage 3: चिकित्सा परीक्षण (Medical Examination)

GIMS Staff Nurse Exam Pattern 2026

Stage / Section Details
Mode & Type Computer Based Test (CBT) - 100 Objective Type MCQs (Total 100 Marks). Paper Bilingual (Hindi & English).
Negative Marking प्रत्येक गलत उत्तर के लिए 0.25 (1/4) अंक की नकारात्मक कटौती (Negative Marking) की जाएगी।
Section A (60 Questions) Mental Health Nursing, Fundamentals, Foundations I&II, First Aid/ACLS/BCLS, Microbiology, Child Health, Nursing Management, Applied Sociology, Medical Surgical Nursing, Pharmacology I&II, Pathology I&II.
Section B (25 Questions) Applied Anatomy/Physiology/Biochemistry, Nutrition & Dietetics, Applied Psychology, Forensic Nursing, Standard Treatment Protocols, Nursing Research & Statistics, Geriatric Nursing.
Section C (15 Questions) Basic Sciences, Communicative English, Community Health Nursing, Environmental Hygiene, Professionalism & Ethics, Health/Nursing Informatics, Obstetrics & Gynaecology.
