IAF AFCAT 02/2026 : Vacancy Details

PostVacancyQualification
AFCAT Entry (Flying & Ground Duty) / NCC Special Entry / GATE Score Entry379+ Posts
Regular / Short Service Commission (SSC) / Permanent Commission (PC)
  • Flying Branch: 10+2 with Physics & Math (min 50% each) AND Graduation (min 60%) or BE/B.Tech (min 60%).
  • Ground Duty (Technical): 10+2 with Physics & Math (min 50% each) AND 4-year Engineering Degree or Integrated PG (min 60%).
  • Ground Duty (Non-Technical): Graduation / PG Degree in relevant stream (WS, Adm, Lgs, Accts, Edn, Met) with minimum 60% marks (50% for PG in Education).

IAF AFCAT 02/2026 Selection Process

  • Stage 1: Online Written Exam (AFCAT) - Exempted for NCC Special Entry & GATE Score Entry
  • Stage 2: Air Force Selection Board (AFSB) Interview (Stage-I & Stage-II Tests)
  • Stage 3: Medical Examination
  • CPSS (Computerised Pilot Selection System) testing for Flying Branch candidates.

IAF AFCAT 02/2026 Exam Pattern

StageDetails
AFCAT Written ExamGeneral Awareness, Verbal Ability in English, Numerical Ability, Reasoning & Military Aptitude Test (100 Questions, 300 Marks, 2 Hours, English Only, +3 for correct, -1 for incorrect).
AFSB Interview (Stage-I)Officer Intelligence Rating Test, Picture Perception & Discussion Test (conducted on Day 1).
AFSB Interview (Stage-II)Psychological Test, Group Tests, and Interview (conducted over the next 5 days).
